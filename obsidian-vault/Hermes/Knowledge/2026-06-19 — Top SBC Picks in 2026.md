## Takeaway

For a low-power embedded product destined for production, the **Particle Tachion** is the strongest choice due to its integrated 5G LTE and Wi-Fi 6 (eliminating external modems), 12 TOPS AI accelerator for on-device inference, and Particle OS cloud platform providing fleet management, over-the-air updates, and remote debugging out of the box. The Raspberry Pi 5 remains viable for prototyping or general-purpose edge nodes where cellular isn't needed, but its lack of built-in connectivity and cloud infrastructure means you build and maintain everything yourself. Nvidia Jetson Orin is overkill unless you need serious vision or robotics inference (40-275 TOPS at 7-60W). In short: match the board to your connectivity, power, and cloud needs — don't let hobbyist specs drive a production decision.
